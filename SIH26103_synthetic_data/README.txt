SIH26103 SYNTHETIC DATASET
===========================
SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT OFFICIAL PAIMANA/MoSPI DATA

Purpose:
Prototype and model-development dataset for SIH26103 Project Intelligence & Risk Engine.

Volume:
- 750 synthetic infrastructure projects
- 24 monthly reporting snapshots per project = 18,000 project-month records
- Additional milestones, issues, outcomes, identity mappings, failure cases,
  source provenance and data-quality events

Design:
- Correlated latent project behavior was used ONLY to generate realistic synthetic trajectories.
- The latent profile is NOT included as an application/ML feature.
- Healthy/moderate/at-risk/critical behaviors differ in progress, expenditure divergence,
  delay, issue pressure and risk trajectory.
- Outcome labels are synthetic and must not be interpreted as official government outcomes.

Important:
- This dataset is for demonstration/model development only.
- It must never be presented as observed PAIMANA/MoSPI data.
- D08 and D11 are schema placeholders for later pipeline phases.
